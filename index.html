Codex Notes

  • Step = rarity: S = 3-star (highest), A = 2-star, B = 1-star, C = 0-star.
  • Alter vs Standard: alter units (suffix _NNN, e.g. M53301_001) are skinned variants with distinct elements/kits. Treated as separate units in gacha optimizer and team builder.
  • Upcoming alters (loc-only, no DataTable row yet) are excluded from this publish.
